## Voices (AI-generated)

Every voice is generated by text to speech (ElevenLabs) with stock voices of adult voice actors: Lily (ariadne), Bill (machine), George (narrator), Alice (note).
No voice imitates a real person; the words of real people are shown as text. Every voice file carries the tag "AI-generated voice (ElevenLabs)".
