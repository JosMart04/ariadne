# AI / SI disclosure

AI assisted this project in the following ways:

- Claude assisted code, interface implementation, simulations, content drafts and testing workflows.
- OpenAI Codex assisted source checking, mission-file drafts, submission documents and repository validation.
- ElevenLabs generated narration and fictional machine speech using stock voices. Generated files are declared in credits and labelled in their metadata.
- Browser speech synthesis reads lines that do not have a recorded voice file.

No real person's voice was cloned. Machine dialogue and the archive guide are fictional and labelled IMAGINED.
Historical claims use linked sources. AI source checks are recorded in `note`; the `verified` field is reserved for human initials.
An empty `verified` field means human sign-off is still pending.

The project uses NASA photographs and official models, not AI-generated images of real machines or places.
Procedural scenery is an artistic reconstruction and carries an on-screen label. Sound effects are synthesized by the application.

The team supplies the concept, chooses the experience and is responsible for final review.
AI assistance and automated tests do not replace human factual, accessibility or rights review.
