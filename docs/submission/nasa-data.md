# NASA data and visual resources

Data & imagery: NASA. This inventory covers the sixteen mission files and the delivered visual assets.
Source-check notes live beside the facts; human approval is separate and remains pending.
NASA mission records supply launch dates, operations, findings and end-of-contact histories.
NASA photographs and official models provide the archive exhibits. Procedural scenery is labelled as an artistic view.
